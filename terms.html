Bridge Review Panel Terms of Use

Terms for the public automated second-opinion workflow.

Effective September 30, 2026

Purpose

The plugin provides independent automated second-opinion reviews. Results may contain mistakes and should be evaluated in context; the plugin does not guarantee correctness or completeness.

Your content

Only submit content you have the right to process. Do not submit credentials, payment-card information, protected health information, government identifiers, MFA codes, or other restricted data.

Third-party processing

Requested reviews are processed using authorized external AI APIs. Availability and response quality can be affected by those services' rate limits, outages, and policies.

High-stakes use

The plugin is not a substitute for qualified professional judgment in medical, legal, financial, safety-critical, or other high-stakes decisions.

Financial risk — no investment advice

Nothing produced by AI Bridge, including any optional trading module in the owner’s private app, is investment, financial, legal or tax advice. Automated trading can lose money, up to the full amount allocated to it; past or backtested results do not predict future results. The owner alone decides whether to trade and is responsible for complying with the laws of their jurisdiction, tax reporting, and the terms of any exchange they use — including whether that exchange is permitted to serve residents of their country.

Abuse and availability

Automated abuse, attempts to bypass rate limits or access controls, and use that violates applicable law or service policies are prohibited. The service may temporarily become unavailable when upstream review capacity is exhausted.
