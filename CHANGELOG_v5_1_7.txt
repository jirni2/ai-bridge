AI Bridge v5.1.7

- Code/security file tasks retain evidence-guarded final verification.
- Added Agent Plan UI, quick control, progress copy, result metadata and Ukrainian/English localization.
- Added regression tests for successful planning/execution and malformed-planner recovery.
