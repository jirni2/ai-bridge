RESIDUAL RISKS (NOT FIXED — DISCLOSED)
• KV has no atomic put-if-absent: two truly simultaneous submits on different edge locations
  can both claim a job (pre-existing, documented). A Durable Object would close it.
• Without AI_BRIDGE_JOBS bound, job recovery and learning are BEST EFFORT (same isolate only).
• ctx.waitUntil work is bounded by the Cloudflare plan; very long God Mode runs can exceed it.
  The stale detector then lets the browser restart the job once; it does not make it longer.
• DNS-based SSRF (public name resolving to a private IP) cannot be checked from a Worker;
  Cloudflare egress does not route to private networks, which is the actual mitigation.
• Live behaviour of third-party providers/search engines was not tested in this environment.
