- The live activity summary no longer says “answer received” after HTTP/provider failures.

VERIFIED BEFORE PATCH
- Production was v5.11.99.
- A real Chat request returned HTTP 503 while the activity summary incorrectly reported success.
- The attached 425-entry ZIP was accepted in production; 417 text/code files were read and Deep Scan offered full coverage in 10 parts.

SECURITY
- ZIP entry, decompression-size and compression-ratio protections remain unchanged.
