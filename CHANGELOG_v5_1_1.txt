AI Bridge v5.1.1

- Added Cloudflare Workers AI image engine support through an AI binding.
- Photo AI and Create Image prefer Cloudflare FLUX.2 klein 4B when the binding is present.
- Automatic fallback to fal.ai if Cloudflare image generation fails.
- Clearer exhausted-balance diagnostics and setup guidance.
- Photo edit sends a safe <=500px fallback reference image to Workers AI while preserving the original for fal.ai.
- Connections now shows whether the Cloudflare Workers AI binding is active.
- Studio can render synchronous data-URI results from Workers AI.
- Video tools remain on fal.ai because Workers AI does not expose direct hosted video generation in this integration.
