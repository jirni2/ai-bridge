AI BRIDGE v5.3.6

- Fixes Files & ZIP appearing to hang after a valid 200+ entry project is picked
  on iPhone/mobile Safari.
- Root cause: the client inflated every text entry, then sorted the results and
  discarded everything beyond the 350k context limit.
- ZIP extraction now scans metadata first, prioritizes likely source/config
  files, and inflates only until the context or decompressed-byte budget is full.
- The loop yields between bounded batches so mobile rendering remains responsive.
- All v5.3.5 ZIP-bomb protections and the 512-entry cap remain enabled.
