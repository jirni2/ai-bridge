TRADING BOT v2 (binance-bot/):
- Native STOP_LOSS_LIMIT placed after every BUY (config stop/limit);
  re-placed on safety buys, cancel/replace, never duplicated.
- alarm() keeps firing while position/pending open (no silent unmonitored
  positions). New `reconcile` command: re-syncs state with exchange,
  clears stale pending, unpauses when flat.
- SELL uses minNotional from exchangeInfo filters (was hardcoded 5 USDT).
- NEW risk.mjs (deterministic, vetoes any entry order): 1% risk/trade
  position sizing; max 10% per coin / 30% total exposure; daily halt at
  -3% (to 00:00 UTC), -5% two days -> 7-day pause; -15% drawdown -> full
  stop + manual review; martingale banned architecturally (mult<=1.5,
  steps<=5); cooldowns 60s / 15min after loss; max 20 trades/day;
  kill-switch `stop-all`; serverTime sync; 429/418 exponential backoff.
- NEW strategies.mjs + indicators.mjs (EMA/RSI/ATR/ADX, no deps):
  regime filter (trend/range/chaos; chaos = volatility >3x avg -> pause);
  BoundedGrid (ADX<20, 12 ATR-sized levels, no martingale, global stop on
  >5% range break); TrendFollow (EMA20/50 cross + RSI>55 + volume, 2.5xATR
  trailing); ConservativeDCA (BTC/ETH only, 7% base, 5x safety -2% x1.25,
  TP +2.5%, HARD -20% stop).
- NEW tools/backtest.mjs: 2y Binance 1h klines, 0.1% fee + 0.07% slippage,
  walk-forward 12mo->3mo, param sweep +-20%, auto-reject on overfit flags
  (winrate>80%, Sharpe>3, maxDD<2%).
  Backtest ConservativeDCA/BTCUSDT: VERDICT FAIL-OVERFIT (winrate 95.2%,
  avg OOS Sharpe -0.46, 2y equity $9779 from $10000). Honest result kept:
  strategy NOT recommended for live; paper/testnet only.
- GET /api/trade/metrics: winRate, profitFactor, sharpe, maxDrawdown,
  calmar, avgWin/avgLoss, currentDrawdown, dailyPnl vs limit, tradesToday,
  monthlyPnl, equityCurve, openPositions.
- Live triple-gate UNCHANGED (BINANCE_LIVE_ENABLED=1 + confirm="LIVE" +
  separate keys). Default: paper/testnet.
- trade/ UI: new Metrics tab (10 cards, SVG equity chart, monthly bars,
  reconcile / stop-all / pause-resume buttons).
