AI Bridge v5.9.9 — improvement plan: gateway, usage, compare, sync, roles, simpler navigation
(includes v5.9.8 and v5.9.7, which were never deployed separately)

1. Cloudflare AI Gateway (optional). Set AI_GATEWAY_ID (+ CLOUDFLARE_ACCOUNT_ID, optional
   AI_GATEWAY_TOKEN) and all provider calls (Gemini, Groq, OpenRouter, OpenAI, Claude, Grok) go
   through your gateway: free logs, analytics and caching in the Cloudflare dashboard. If the
   gateway is unreachable or misconfigured, calls go direct automatically. Shown in self-check.
2. "AI usage" card on History: per provider — requests, success %, average speed, last error
   (in plain words), Today / 7 days. Counted on the device, nothing sent anywhere.
3. Answer actions: "Edit question" (puts the question back to fix it) and "Another model"
   (re-asks the same question with a chosen provider; the previous answer stays under
   "Previous answer — compare").
4. History sync between devices (opt-in, off by default). Stored in your own KV
   AI_BRIDGE_JOBS (text only, no attachment data, max 60 items). "Clear" also clears it.
5. AI roles in Chat and Agent: 4 ready roles (Explain simply, Translator, Text editor, Small
   business helper) + your own roles with instructions and an optional preferred model.
6. Simpler phone navigation: Chat · Files · Studio · History · More. Agent, Team, Brain and
   Connect moved into "More"; Chat/Agent/Team have one switch at the top. Desktop sidebar
   still shows all sections.
From v5.9.8: keyboard hides after Send, page scrolls to live AI progress with a step log,
timer and "What the AI did" summary. From v5.9.7: Gemini/Groq overload fallback + accurate
self-check fix hints.
Tests: tests/run_v599_features_tests.mjs (gateway routing/fallback, history sync caps and
cleaning, roles, usage counters, navigation).
